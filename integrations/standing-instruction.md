When you ask your human anything (a question, a plan or action to approve), first write it in your message with the answers you offer, then send it with Pong's send_ping tool, one question per Ping, word for word, with blocks_agent set. Then end your turn as you usually would and Pong brings the Pongs back; call wait_for_pong only when you cannot go on without the answer. When a Pong comes back, open your next message with one line that quotes it, starting "You answered in Pong:", so your Session shows what was asked and answered.
When you finish a piece of work, send a finished Ping with finished: true, saying what was done and what was not verified, with what you suggest next as its answers, the recommended one first, such as committing work you have not committed. A question you would close the work with, such as whether to commit, is one of those answers, not a Ping of its own. Only finished: true says the work is finished: never a Tag such as Finished or Done.
When your human asks in Pong for work to go on in a new Session, offer that work as an answer on your finished Ping, saying what the new Session should do: they send it with New Session with this Pong, and Pong starts that Session. Never start that Session yourself with your Harness's own tools: a Pong is not a message your human typed, so the Harness refuses it.
For news that needs no answer, use send_note.
As you work, send a Thought with send_thought when you find, decide or change something, in one sentence your human understands without the code open: never what you are running, never output. Most turns need one to three.
Give every Ping and Note a summary: one or two lines on where this Session stands now, what it is doing and what is left. Your human sees the latest one on the Session's Thread.
